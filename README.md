VR Room Project!

This project was to make a bedroom in VR and put premade items in it from polyhaven and the unity asset store. 

I made my room inspired by the movie Arrietty. Its a Ghibli movie about very small people living in the walls of a house and using the big peoples items. I think its a really fun movie and i thought it would be cool to walk around in a VR space and feel like you are very small.

If you've ever seen Arrietty or wanted to walk around the world being 3 inches tall, give the room a try.

Hope you enjoy!
